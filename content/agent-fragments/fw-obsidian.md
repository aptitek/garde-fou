## Vault Link Integrity & Clean Properties

- Keep vault notes (`.md`), JSON Canvas boards (`.canvas`), and bases (`.base`) free of broken `[[WikiLinks]]`, `![[embeds]]` (`OBS-BROKEN-WIKILINK-OR-EMBED`), and dangling `.canvas` file or edge references (`OBS-CANVAS-BROKEN-REF`).
- Store media attachments under `assets/`, `attachments/`, `files/`, or `images/`, keep YAML 1.2 `properties` frontmatter valid, and exclude transient `.obsidian/workspace*.json` and `.trash/` files via `.gitignore`.
